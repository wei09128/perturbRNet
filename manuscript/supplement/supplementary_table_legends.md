# Supplementary table titles and legends

## Table S1. Simulation scenarios and performance

Simulation designs and performance summaries for end-to-end count generation, identifiability, estimator comparison and prior-sign uncertainty. Raw scenario-level outputs are distributed separately as machine-readable supplementary data.

## Table S2. Norman threshold and expression sensitivity

Sensitivity of the Norman hidden-cancellation endpoint and perturbRNet discrimination to response thresholds and gene expression. Extreme threshold cells with few positives are descriptive.

## Table S3. Dataset, sample and analysis-design summary

Datasets, cohorts, samples and validation partitions used in the NASA, Alzheimer-model and Norman analyses. Norman cell partitions and gemgroups are technical partitions rather than biological replicates.

## Table S4. Alzheimer-model restoration candidates and calibration

Expression-restoration classifications, degree calibration and matched conditional-null results for AZD and shATM. Candidate genes are mechanistic hypotheses rather than validated causal mediators.

## Table S5. NASA comparison-level perturbRNet fits and null controls

NASA young-cortex and old-whole-brain comparison-level perturbRNet fits, identifier mapping and 200-draw destroyed-information controls. Cohorts were analyzed independently.

## Table S6. Norman held-out validation and robustness analyses

Primary balance-constrained Norman validation and sensitivity analyses, including expression-aware matching, capture-partition cross-fitting and discovery-noise-standardized endpoints. Bootstrap resampling units were perturbation pairs.
